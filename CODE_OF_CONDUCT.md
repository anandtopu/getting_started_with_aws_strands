# Code of conduct

This learning project welcomes people with different backgrounds, identities, abilities, and levels of experience. Ask questions freely, explain assumptions, critique the work respectfully, and make room for others to learn.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized conduct, publishing another person's private information, and sustained disruption are unacceptable. Do not share credentials, personal customer records, or private messages in examples or reports.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions, explain the reason, and restrict participation when necessary. Responses should be proportionate: a clarification or warning for a correctable mistake, a temporary restriction for repeated disruption, and removal for serious abuse. Decisions should consider context and offer an opportunity to respond when safe.

Report ordinary participation problems to the repository maintainer through an available private contact channel on their profile. Do not post sensitive details publicly. This local repository does not invent a reporting email or claim to provide a staffed response service. If no private channel is available, a public issue may request private contact without naming people or describing sensitive events.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and events explicitly organized for this course.
