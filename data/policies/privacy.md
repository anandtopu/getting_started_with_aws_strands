# Nimbus Privacy and retention policy

POL-PRI-001 | Synthetic edition 2027-01-01 | USD

All course identities and transactions are fabricated. Do not enter real passport numbers, payment cards, passwords or customer data into these lessons.

Local conversation history is retained for 30 days by the teaching policy. Travel preferences expire after 90 days unless the synthetic user renews consent.

A deletion request removes the user's local conversation and preference records within 24 hours in the simulated workflow. Deletion includes indexes and cached preference copies, not just the user-facing display.

Audit logs contain a synthetic actor ID, action, result and time. Raw passport-like and card-like strings are redacted before logging. Audit retention is 7 days.

Memory is scoped to a user and purpose. A tool must not fetch another user's preferences merely because a model asks it to.
