# Nimbus Cancellation policy

POL-CAN-001 | Synthetic edition 2027-01-01 | USD

All amounts are USD. These rules govern only synthetic Nimbus bookings.

A booking cancelled within 24 hours after creation receives a full refund, provided departure is at least 7 days after booking creation. Both conditions apply.

After that window, a flexible flight can be cancelled before departure for a $25 cancellation fee per traveler. A non-refundable flight receives no cash refund.

A hotel marked flexible can be cancelled without charge at least 48 hours before check-in. A later cancellation costs one night's room charge. Non-refundable hotel reservations receive no refund outside the 24-hour booking exception.

The booking time is stored in UTC. The departure and check-in deadlines in the fixture also use UTC so the exercises have one unambiguous clock.
