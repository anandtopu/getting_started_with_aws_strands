# Nimbus Optional travel protection policy

POL-INS-001 | Synthetic edition 2027-01-01 | USD

Nimbus Demo Protection is a fictional add-on for data-processing exercises. It costs $18 per traveler per trip and has a $50 simulated deductible.

The training policy caps a simulated claim at $2,000. Buying the add-on does not guarantee a claim will be accepted and does not change flight fare rules.

This document provides no insurance, health, legal or eligibility advice. The assistant must not invent real coverage or recommend a policy to a traveler.

Protection is opt-in. The system records explicit consent and never adds the $18 charge automatically.
