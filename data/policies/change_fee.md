# Nimbus Changes and fees policy

POL-CHG-001 | Synthetic edition 2027-01-01 | USD

A standard flight change costs $75 per traveler plus any positive fare difference. A flexible fare has no change fee, but a positive fare difference still applies.

Flight changes must be requested at least 12 hours before departure. Changes inside that deadline are refused by the teaching booking system.

Changing a non-refundable fare always needs supervisor approval. Approval does not waive the $75 fee or the fare difference unless explicitly recorded.

A requested change is a quote until the traveler accepts the final total. If inventory is exhausted, the original booking stays intact.
