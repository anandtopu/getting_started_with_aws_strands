# Nimbus Baggage policy

POL-BAG-001 | Synthetic edition 2027-01-01 | USD

Each synthetic Nimbus flight includes one cabin bag up to 8 kg and one personal item up to 3 kg per traveler.

The first checked bag costs $30 each way and may weigh at most 23 kg. A second checked bag costs $45 each way, with the same 23 kg limit.

A checked bag over 23 kg and up to 32 kg incurs an additional $60 charge. Items above 32 kg are not accepted by the synthetic booking policy.

These are fictional commercial limits for course exercises, not guidance for any real airline or journey.
